# Business Problem Statement

## Overview

Retail businesses generate large volumes of transactional data across customers, products, regions, and sales channels. However, without proper visualization and analytical tools, identifying meaningful business insights becomes difficult.

This project aims to solve that problem by creating an interactive Tableau dashboard capable of transforming raw sales data into actionable business intelligence.

---

# Problem Statement

Business stakeholders often struggle to answer critical operational and strategic questions such as:

- Which product categories generate the highest sales?
- Which regions contribute the most revenue?
- Which customer segments are most profitable?
- Which managers are driving business growth?
- Which products or sub-categories are underperforming?
- How are sales and profitability changing over time?

Traditional spreadsheet-based analysis makes it difficult to:
- identify patterns quickly
- monitor KPIs efficiently
- compare multiple business dimensions simultaneously

---

# Objective

The objective of this dashboard is to provide:
- centralized KPI monitoring
- interactive sales analysis
- geographical business insights
- customer performance tracking
- category-level profitability analysis

The dashboard enables faster and more informed business decision-making through visual analytics.
